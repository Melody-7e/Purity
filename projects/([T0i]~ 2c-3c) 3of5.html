So, it's a cover for Shamir's Secret Sharing which let you divide some confidential data into $N$ shares such that $K$ many of them can recover the secret (but not less than $K$). So even if $k - 1 of shares got public or $n - $k got lost it won't matter.

I loved 2 of 3, it has a nice offline decorated script to recover secret and decorated image based cards too. I wanted to use that for my `purpose` but I need 3 of 5, not 2 of 3 (and it doesn't have dynamic parameter for that, at least at not at the time I was checking). So I created my own, instead of decorated image card, I made a decorated html card for each share (so they all hold a script to recover them) [though it doesn't look beautiful as no gallery wants to show preview of a potential malware HTML] and it also includes an CRC-32 hash to maintain the integrity of the secret.

I am bad at thinking names. So it's called 3 of 5.

Tips and Tricks

In case you want to split very large data (don't use it directly, it just freezes on my PC), you can encrypt your data via something like AES (like just use an encrypted 7z archive) and use a randomly generated password. Then split the password using this... Done~

Well it *can* safely spit its own code (30KB), but don't complain that each share is 150KB (I can fix that, but I am lazzy), so just compress that if size matters.

Security

I do love to code all by myself, but I really don't like writing cryptographical stuff. So I just used Grempe's secrets.js, so refer to that. Also, the CRC-32 hash is calculated by mixing the secret with its UUID first, so even if you have two identical secrets, the hash won't be identical.

Important: The CRC-32 of the `secret` is saved in each share, so don't have the `secret` small enough to be recoverable by its CRC. (Make it at least 8 letters long, otherwise delete the CRC manually).
